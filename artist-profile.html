Commercial & Editorial Photographer

Chanda Mwewa is a Lusaka-based commercial photographer with over 8 years of experience capturing compelling visuals for brands, events, weddings and editorial campaigns across Zambia. Known for a distinct aesthetic that blends vibrant Zambian culture with modern visual storytelling, Chanda has worked with leading brands, top musicians and high-profile events throughout the country.

With a keen eye for light and composition, Chanda delivers consistently high-quality imagery that tells authentic stories. Available for corporate assignments, branded content, live events and portrait sessions.
