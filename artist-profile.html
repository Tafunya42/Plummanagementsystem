About

Chanda & The Soul Collective is a 5-piece Afro-Soul live band rooted in Lusaka, Zambia. We blend contemporary Afrobeats, soulful R&B, and traditional Zambian rhythms into an unforgettable live experience. With over a decade of collective performance across corporate galas, private weddings, and festivals, we bring warmth, energy, and world-class musicianship to every stage. Our repertoire spans original compositions to tasteful covers — curated for your event's mood.
